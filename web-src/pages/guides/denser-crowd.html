Simulating a denser crowd

The test files have more S2/S3 records per S1 record than the training files (5.76 vs. 4.67). --sim-drop lets you train and tune in a similarly crowded setting. 

python scripts/train.py --sim-drop 0.2

This removes the given share of S1 records outside the validation set from the index. Their S2/S3 records stay, and now match nothing, so they become distractors that look like real businesses. Validation S1 records are never removed. With 0.2, the distractor share rises from 26% to 40%.

In the reported experiment, validation scored 0.9850 against 0.9861 without it, and the chosen threshold barely moved. The removed businesses' records are loose distractors, while the hard cases are near-copies of businesses that are in the index. The option is off by default.
