The lessons are more useful than the numbers:

  • More candidates were not the answer. Raising blocking recall from 98.27% to 98.45% gave the matcher candidates it could not use.
  • Reading the errors was. The error examples pointed at house numbers and at same-name neighbours. The features built from that (step 3) were the biggest single improvement, mostly in recall.
  • A small, targeted expensive model pays off. The cross-encoder only sees about 0.7% of pairs, yet added +0.003.
